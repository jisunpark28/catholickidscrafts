## Overview

**Duration:** 25–35 minutes (classroom) or 15 minutes (at-home coloring)  
**Season:** Lent, especially Weeks 1–4

## Learning goals

- Name **Stations of the Cross** as a Lenten prayer walk with Jesus
- Connect purple season with **penance** and **mercy**
- Take home one finished station or a booklet cover for family prayer

## Supplies (per child)

- Printed Stations page or booklet template (see TPT pack for full set)
- Crayons or colored pencils (purple, brown, gray)
- Scissors and stapler if making a mini-booklet
- Optional: purple ribbon to tie booklet

## Steps

1. Briefly explain: *We remember Jesus' path to the cross so our hearts grow more loving.*
2. Color one station (or cover + Station I) with care—not a race.
3. Invite a quiet prayer: *Jesus, I love you. Help me follow you.*
4. Staple or fold; send home with parent note to pray one station on Friday.

## Gospel connection

Use the Sunday Gospel from Lent Week 1 (temptation in the desert) or Mark 8:34 — *take up your cross.*

## Take-home

Pair with the Lesson Kit **at-home link**: one station colored + one sentence Lent promise at dinner.

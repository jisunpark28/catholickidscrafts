## Overview

**Duration:** 40–50 minutes  
**Season:** Advent (also works in late November catechesis)

## Learning goals

- Name the four weeks of Advent
- Explain that Advent is a time of **hope** and preparation for Christmas
- Connect the wreath candles to prayer at home

## Supplies (per child)

- Printed wreath template on card stock
- Purple and pink crayons or paper strips
- Scissors, glue stick
- Flame shapes (yellow paper) or draw with marker

## Steps

1. Color three candles purple and one pink (Gaudete Sunday).
2. Cut out wreath and candle pieces; glue to a paper plate base if desired.
3. Each week, “light” one flame (attach or flip up) during opening prayer.

## Gospel connection

Read Luke 1:26–38 or the Sunday Gospel for Advent I–IV depending on your calendar.

## Take-home

Send a one-page parent note: how to pray one decade of the Rosary or a simple Advent prayer around the wreath.

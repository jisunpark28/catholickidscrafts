## Before you begin

This lesson supports **First Reconciliation** preparation. Keep language positive—focus on God's mercy, not fear.

## Discussion (10 minutes)

Explain that examining our conscience means thinking quietly about how we have loved God and others, and where we can do better.

## Guided questions

Use these prompts aloud; allow silence between each:

1. Have I prayed—or remembered to talk to God?
2. Have I been kind to my family and friends?
3. Have I told the truth?
4. Have I shared and taken turns?
5. Is there anything I should say sorry for?

## Activity

Students complete the worksheet in pairs with a catechist circulating. Invite volunteers to share **one** thing they are thankful for—not private sins.

## Closing

Remind children: *Jesus always welcomes us back.* Practice the Act of Contrition together.

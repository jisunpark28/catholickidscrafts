## Lesson objectives

- Introduce God's promise and care for creation
- Practice fine motor skills with coloring
- Connect the rainbow to God's covenant (age-appropriate)

## Materials

- Printed coloring page (one per child)
- Crayons or colored pencils
- Optional: cotton balls for clouds, glue stick

## Opening (5 minutes)

Gather children and ask: *Who takes care of all the animals?* Briefly tell the story of Noah in simple language.

## Activity (15–20 minutes)

1. Distribute coloring pages.
2. Encourage children to name animals as they color.
3. Add a rainbow with bold strokes—remind them it is a sign of God's promise.

## Closing prayer

*Lord, thank you for caring for every creature. Help us care for others too. Amen.*

## Extension

Pair with a **animal sounds** game or a paper-plate ark craft for a full 45-minute session.
